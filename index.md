## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/profile-pic.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
My name is {{ site.name }}, and I am a mechanical engineering student at Cornell University with a passion for engineering design, problem-solving, and building innovative systems. As the Magnetics Subteam Lead on Cornell's Hyperloop Project Team, I use Ansys Maxwell to design and optimize a linear induction motor for a magnetically levitating pod, while also developing manufacturing-ready hardware and coordinating system integration efforts. Through this work, I have gained experience in electromagnetic simulation, electromechanical system design, CAD, prototyping, and engineering analysis.

During the summer of 2026, I worked as an Engineering Intern at X-Ray Optical Systems, where I contributed to engineering projects in a professional development environment and gained experience working alongside multidisciplinary teams to solve technical challenges. This experience strengthened my understanding of the engineering design process and exposed me to the standards and practices used in industry.

My technical background includes SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Onshape, MATLAB, Python, additive manufacturing, machining, and product development. I enjoy taking projects from initial concepts through testing and validation, combining analytical problem-solving with hands-on engineering. In addition to my design experience, I have served as a Learning Assistant for Cornell's calculus-based physics courses, helping students develop a deeper understanding of mechanics and electromagnetism.

Outside of the classroom, I co-founded the Adirondack Solar Alliance, a nonprofit that has raised over $100,000 to bring renewable energy solutions to communities in Puerto Rico. I’m also passionate about 3D printing, sustainable engineering, and exploring new technologies that push boundaries. In my free time a really enjoy mountain biking, skiing, cliff jumping, playing soccer, and pickleball.
