## Fluid-Mechanical Dissection
The Fluid-Mechanical Dissection is an end-of-term project as a part of MAE 3230 Introductory Fluid Mechanics. As a group of five, we are assigned a device to take apart with shop tools, document this disassembly process with a video, and create a separate video on the underlying fluid mechanics of one of the components of the device.

My group was assigned a Honda Weed Whacker/Trimmer.
